FUNDING BASICS / November 7, 2026

Transactional funding, without the jargon.

The role short-term capital can play between a property purchase and an onward sale.

Transactional funding is short-term capital used for a specific real estate transaction. A buyer may need funds to complete a purchase before an onward sale provides the proceeds intended to repay that funding.

This can be relevant to a double closing: two separate transactions, a purchase and a resale. The exact sequence and legal requirements depend on the transaction and location.

It is not the same as a mortgage for a long-term hold or a renovation facility. A clear exit plan, reliable closing arrangements and suitable documentation are important.

Before proceeding, discuss eligibility, repayment obligations, timing and any fees in writing. This article is general information, not legal advice or an offer of funding.
