GLOSSARY

Speak the language.

A–B / B–C
The two legs of a double close: you buy from the seller (A–B), then sell to your end buyer (B–C).
Spread
The difference between your purchase price and your resale price. It often repays short-term funding.
Seller carry
Part of the price the seller lets you pay later through a note, instead of in cash at closing.
EMD
Earnest money deposit: the good-faith money that secures a purchase contract.
Primary lender
The lender that funds the largest share of the purchase, in first position. Don’t have one? We can provide one.
ARV
After-repair value: what the property should be worth once the work is finished.
LTV / LTC
Loan-to-value and loan-to-cost: how big the loan is compared with the property’s value or the total project cost.
Title & escrow
The neutral company that holds funds, checks ownership and records the closing. Our funds only move through them.
