CLOSING NOTES / November 7, 2026

Three questions to ask about a double closing.

Timing, documentation and repayment: the details worth discussing early.

First, how will the two closings be coordinated? Discuss the sequence, expected timing and dependencies with the closing professionals involved.

Second, which documents and approvals are required? Requirements vary, so ask your legal and closing advisers rather than relying on a generic checklist alone.

Third, what happens if the onward sale does not close as planned? Understand repayment responsibilities and any risks under the proposed agreement.

Transactional funding is transaction-specific. Get the applicable terms in writing and seek independent advice before making a commitment.
