HOW IT WORKS

The deal drives the conversation.

Transactional funding supports the purchase leg of a real estate transaction where an onward sale or exit is already planned. Repayment is intended to come from that exit.

  1. 01

    You buy the property

    Our short-term capital funds the purchase leg, or the gap in it, of a transaction where the exit is already planned.

  2. 02

    Funds go to title

    Money is only ever wired to the title or escrow company, under disbursement instructions agreed before anything moves.

  3. 03

    Repaid at closing

    Repayment comes from the onward sale, the seller carry or the spread, on the settlement statement, often the same day.

What it is, and what it isn’t

It is

Short-term capital tied to one transaction, with timing and a clear exit at the centre of the review.

✕ It isn’t

A mortgage, renovation loan or long-term investment loan.

What to bring to an enquiry

  • Purchase and resale agreements, if available

How much do you charge?

Every deal is priced individually. It depends on the structure, the amount and the timeline, and your exact costs come in your written terms before closing. A couple of guidelines:
• EMD funding starts at $7,000 (the smallest EMD amount we fund).
• Double closes under $150,000 have a fixed $1,500 fee, paid at closing.

What qualifies as transactional funding?

A double closing is a real estate transaction method where two back-to-back property sales occur on the same day, involving three parties: the original seller, the investor (middleman), and the end buyer. The investor agrees to purchase the property from the original seller, then simultaneously sells it to the end buyer at a higher price, using the end buyer’s funds to complete the purchase. This allows the investor to profit from the difference without using their own funds for an extended period.

Can you fund EMD for end buyers?

We can, but it’s rare. Our funds have to be protected no matter what, typically through a refundable period (inspection period). If your deal has no inspection period or the EMD is non-refundable, we will not fund it. We will provide the proper addendums for your purchase contract to keep our funds safe and your deal moving forward.

Is there a max amount you can fund?

There is no limit on the funds available for EMD, Double Closings, or Stack Method deals. As long as your deal qualifies under our standards, we can be your one-stop shop for all transactional funding.

How quickly can we get our deal funded?

We typically require 48 hours of notice to fund a deal, however we have funded in as quickly as 5 minutes. If you have a deal, submit it as soon as possible so we can review it and get the process started.

What happens if the deal doesn’t close?

If your deal does not close, we do not charge the transaction fees. Your only cost would be any non-refundable up-front fees, since the work has already been done to put funds aside or fund your deal.

What is your role in a Stack Method deal?

As your 3rd party funding partner we: ensure we meet your primary lender’s down payment sourcing and seasoning requirements; protect our funds throughout the transaction; bring the down payment funds to close with your primary lender; ensure Title is a party to our irrevocable disbursement instructions; record and disburse funds with our in-house attorney; and handle TC, account setup, contracts & security docs, Title coordination, Seller note recording, and more.

Are rates or funding amounts guaranteed?

No. Eligibility, amounts, fees and funding timing depend on an individual review and written agreement. No specific terms are offered on this website.

How do I make a payment?

Only pay an applicable service or transaction amount once it has been agreed in writing. Payment instructions are included with your written terms. A consultation booking is not a funding disbursement.

DEAL TYPES

Four ways to get funded.

Each structure has its own guide: what it is, when to use it, and how we fund it. Learn it, then start your request.

Stack Method

A primary loan plus seller carry, stacked into one purchase. Close with little or none of your own cash, sometimes with cash back.

Echo Method

Your end buyer is short on the down payment? We cover it, and the deal’s spread repays us at the same closing.

Double Close

Funding for the A–B purchase in a back-to-back closing, repaid from the B–C sale the same day. Your spread stays private.

EMD

Earnest money funded before your deadline, so you can lock up a contract without tying up your own capital.

Don’t have a primary lender? We can provide one.

Many deals need a lender for the largest share of the price. Tick “I need a primary lender” in your request and we’ll bring one into your deal.
